DESIGN PRINCIPLES APPLIED
--------------------------
- White/cream space is luxury: all sections use py-20 or py-24
- Serif headings everywhere: Cormorant Garamond font-light
- Rose gold (#C4A882) replaces all red accents
- Smooth transitions: duration-300 ease-out on all hovers
- Subtle shadows only: shadow-sm, never shadow-lg
- Cards float without borders: product grid uses gap, not card outlines
- Typography hierarchy: 10px uppercase stone captions → 14px font-display product names → 48px display headings
